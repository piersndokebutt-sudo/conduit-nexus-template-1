Critical natural resources

Diversification is the new status quo.

The challenge

More than one route to supply.

Critical resource supply chains are rarely simple, and they have always been exposed to disruption. Organisations at every stage of the market have reason to look beyond their existing relationships, now more than ever.

Those who want more than one route face the same challenge: finding trusted partners who understand the macroeconomic context and can reduce their exposure in markets where they have a limited footprint.

The real cost

Diversifying is sensible. Building the credible network of partners to support it is where businesses most often lose time and money.
